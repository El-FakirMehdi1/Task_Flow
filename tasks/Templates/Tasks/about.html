About TaskFlow

Built to turn busy notes into calm, actionable plans.

v1.0 daily planning tool

Why it exists

TaskFlow is designed for people who want a clean, fast, and personal way to manage priorities. Instead of cluttered dashboards, it gives you a focused system for tracking tasks, grouping work by category, and monitoring progress without losing momentum.

The interface follows a practical paper-and-logbook aesthetic—direct, readable, and calm—so your to-do list feels less overwhelming and more intentional.

What you can do

  • Organize tasks into categories
  • Track priorities and completion status
  • Review a quick dashboard overview
  • Stay focused with a simple, clean workflow
